Limitation Lock

Every case has a clock. You get an event and a date. Pick the last day you can still file. Miss it and the door locks. Dates are new every round.

How the counting works
  • The day the period starts from is left out (Section 12(1), Limitation Act). A 30-day period from 1 March ends on 31 March.
  • Years and months run to the same date in the later year or month.
  • If the last day falls on a day the court is closed, you can file on the day it reopens (Section 4). Here that means Monday.
  • Simplified for the game: no holidays other than Sundays, and no time spent getting certified copies.
