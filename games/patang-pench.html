Patang Pench

A break from the courtroom. It is kite season over the old city. Steer your kite into a pench (cross another kite's string), then pull hard before they do. Cut a kite and the rooftops shout "Bo kaata!"

Score0
Kites cut0
Kites left3
Time90

Steer with the mouse, your finger, or the arrow keys. When strings cross, a ring appears around your kite: pull with a quick swipe, the Pull button, or Space.

Basant on the rooftops

Three kites. Ninety seconds.

Each cut is +100, more if you chain them. If a pench runs out before you pull, your kite is cut instead.
