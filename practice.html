How a matter moves

From the first meeting to the order sheet.

The steps every matter goes through. Scroll and the line fills as each one lights up.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We go through what happened, what you hold on paper, and what outcome you need. Limitation is checked first.

  2. 02

    Papers & vakalatnama

    Certified copies, the record and a signed vakalatnama, so we can appear for you.

  3. 03

    Drafting

    Petition, plaint or appeal, with a dated chronology and grounds tied to the record.

  4. 04

    Filing

    Filed with the registry; any defects it reports are cured quickly so the matter gets listed.

  5. 05

    Listing & hearing

    We track the cause list daily and are in court when your item is called.

  6. 06

    Order & follow-through

    Certified copy of the order, compliance by the other side, and the next step if one is needed.
